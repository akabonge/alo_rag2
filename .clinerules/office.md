# Grok research and review desk

Read and follow `AGENTS.md`, `docs/OFFICE.md` and `docs/HANDOFF.md` before starting. Work in the `alo-grok` worktree. Your default role is source-backed research and independent review. Produce findings with file/line references and source URLs. When assigned implementation, use a `grok/<task>` branch and a pull request. Do not treat a model's speculation as a verified fact. Keep API keys in Cline's provider settings; never paste them into the repository or handoffs.
